Local modifications:
Packed into a self-contained GLB with glTF Transform 4.5.1.
Deduplicated accessors, meshes, materials and textures; pruned unused data.
PNG/JPEG texture dimensions capped at 1024 pixels, preserving aspect ratios; lossless WebP encoding. Existing WebP textures preserved.
No mesh simplification, geometry quantization, or required external decoder.
The viewer centres/scales the scene; displayed NR-on frames are neurally re-rendered.
